Experiments 06 to 08 (cuRobo planning on the SAM 3 depth cloud)

One run per cell, 43 rollouts. Solved: exp06 (no scene text) 1 of 3, exp07 (perceived scene) 2 of 3, exp08 (simulator scene) 2 of 3. Of 182 failed move_to_pose calls, 142 were "goal state infeasible (scene_collision)", 22 other cuRobo refusals, 10 timeouts (cause not investigated), 8 "episode ended".

Why: the bowl in the gripper is not attached to the robot and none of the 43 programs passes it as target_point_cloud, so its depth points stay in the cloud, 10 to 18 cm under the hand flange (median 14 cm). A goal that lowers the hand moves the hand into them and cuRobo refuses it.

Toggle below: before = as in the run; after = the bowl's points removed and replaced by spheres attached to the hand. This is a replay with my own collision check, not the real cuRobo (the service may not accept attached objects), fitted after seeing the failures: 13 of 15 refused plans in the failed run have the hand inside the cloud at the goal, none collide after attaching; in the successful run 6 of 9 still touch because the bowl is lowered onto the plate.
